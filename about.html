Margaret (Meg) Conkey is Professor Emerita in the Department of Anthropology at the University of California, Berkeley, where she taught for many years and was closely involved with the Archaeological Research Facility. She received her PhD from the University of Chicago.

An archaeologist of the European Upper Paleolithic, she has carried out long-term fieldwork in the French Pyrenees, through the regional survey Between the Caves and excavations at Peyre Blanque. She is widely known for her work on the interpretation of Paleolithic imagery and as one of the founders of feminist and gender archaeology.
