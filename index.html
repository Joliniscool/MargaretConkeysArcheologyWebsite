Welcome

Archaeology at the intersection of art, place, and people

Meg Conkey is Professor Emerita of Anthropology at the University of California, Berkeley. For more than four decades her work has asked how people of the Upper Paleolithic made meaning in their worlds, and how archaeologists make meaning of them.

Her research ranges from field survey and excavation in the French Pyrenees to the history of how “Paleolithic art” has been interpreted, and to the ways feminist and gender perspectives have reshaped archaeological practice.
