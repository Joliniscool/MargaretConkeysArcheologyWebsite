Field project · 1993–present

About the project

Draft text: replace with Meg's project description. The logo is a placeholder for the BtC FileMaker database logo.

The famous painted caves of the French Pyrenees have long dominated our picture of the Upper Paleolithic. Between the Caves turns attention to the open-air landscape around them: the places where people camped, worked stone, and moved through the land between the caves.

Since 1993 the project has used systematic surface survey, test excavation, and a shared field database to map the traces of Paleolithic life across the Ariège.
